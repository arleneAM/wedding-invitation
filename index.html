Once Upon a Time

Our Story

Some love stories begin with a spark. Ours began long before we knew it, with two paths that crossed, parted, and found their way back to each other in God’s perfect time.

Moments We Treasure

  1. 2019

    The First Encounter

    We met as workmates, unaware that this ordinary encounter would one day become part of an extraordinary love story. Back then, we were simply two people passing through the same chapter, never knowing that life would give us another chance.

  2. 2022

    How We Met… Again

    Fate brought us together once more in Pampanga. This time, conversations turned into laughter, strangers became confidants, and somewhere in the simplicity of our everyday moments, we found something neither of us had been looking for — a love that felt like home.

  3. 2023

    Our First Adventure

    On a whim, we found ourselves in Baguio, where our first adventure became a collection of moments we would carry in our hearts. Amidst the cool breeze and beautiful scenery, we discovered that the best part of any journey was never the destination, but having each other along the way.

  4. 2024

    A Year of Us

    We celebrated our first anniversary in La Union, surrounded by friends and memories that still bring smiles to our faces. It was more than a trip; it was a reminder of how beautifully ordinary moments can become extraordinary when shared with the person you love.

  5. 2025

    The Promise of Forever

    With the help of our dear friends, Paolo asked Arlene to spend forever with him on the shores of Boracay. It was a simple moment, yet it held the promise of a thousand tomorrows — a lifetime of choosing each other, facing the unknown together, and turning every new adventure into a memory of our own.

  6. 2027

    Happily Ever After

    January 8, 2027 — the beginning of forever.

    Before we found each other, we each walked our own roads, carrying battles no one else could fully understand. Life tested us, taught us, and brought us through seasons that required patience, courage, and faith. Looking back, we now understand that even the detours had a purpose, for every road we took led us to this moment, to this love, to each other.

    And so, before God and the people we hold dear, we begin our forever with hearts full of gratitude, knowing that what took years to unfold was never a moment too late. For in His perfect time, two separate journeys became one, and a chance encounter became a lifelong promise.

    We were once two stories unfolding apart. Now, by God’s grace, we begin a lifetime of writing ours together.
